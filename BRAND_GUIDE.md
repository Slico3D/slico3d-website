# SLICO3D Brand Guide

## About

SLICO3D is a German 3D printing brand based in Bavaria.

The company develops practical and durable 3D printed solutions for electricians, networking equipment, workshop organization and custom projects.

Products are designed and manufactured in Bavaria, Germany.

---

## Brand Values

* Practical
* Reliable
* Durable
* Functional
* Honest
* Made in Bavaria

SLICO3D is not a gadget brand.

The focus is solving real problems with well designed 3D printed products.

---

## Brand Voice

Use a friendly and professional tone.

German customers should feel:

* Trust
* Quality
* Reliability
* Precision

Communication should be simple and direct.

Avoid marketing buzzwords.

Avoid exaggerated claims.

Preferred style:

"Praktische 3D-Lösungen. Durchdacht. Robust. Made in Bayern."

---

## Visual Style

Clean and modern.

Inspired by practical workshop tools and restrained technical design. Olive, graphite, sand and silver; small diagonal accents, readable text and real product photos.

Use plenty of whitespace.

Avoid cluttered layouts.

Design should feel premium but approachable.

---

## Color Palette

Website design proposal:

* Graphite background: #141713
* Olive accent: #a5af79
* Sand accent: #d3c5a8
* Off-white text: #eeede6
* Muted text: #b6b8ad

Use a consistent dark appearance in this proposal. Keep actual product colors unchanged. Avoid camouflage, aggressive military imagery, neon colors and decorative chrome effects.

The website palette is a proposal on `design/olive-refresh`; it does not change the existing eBay listing master.

## Homepage Vision

The homepage must immediately communicate:

1. Who we are
2. What we create
3. Why visitors should trust us

Hero message:

"Praktische 3D-Lösungen. Durchdacht. Robust. Made in Bayern."

The homepage should flow naturally:

Hero → product highlights → small-business introduction → workshop/blog → contact.

Link to Instagram without an embedded feed. Use existing real product photos. The primary homepage action opens the SLICO3D product section; eBay is a secondary link while the integrated shop is being prepared.

Avoid a single static company business card page.

The website should feel alive and regularly updated.

## Products

Current focus:

Products sold on eBay should also appear on the website.

## Blog

Blog posts should be written in German.

Tone:

Friendly ("Du")

Topics:

* New products
* 3D printing projects
* Workshop life
* Development stories
* Printing tips
* Behind the scenes

The blog is an important part of the SLICO3D identity.
